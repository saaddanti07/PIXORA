## Real-ESRGAN — restoration weights and architecture

The file `android/app/src/main/assets/models/realesr_general_x4v3_fp32.tflite`
is a conversion of the official `realesr-general-x4v3` checkpoint published by the
Real-ESRGAN project. The conversion is performed by `tool/convert_model.py` from
the unmodified upstream weights, whose SHA-256 is pinned in
`models/weights.sha256`.

* Upstream: <https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN>
* Weights: <https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN/releases/tag/v0.2.5.0>
